# Wild Intervention Validation

Test date: 2026-10-13. Nifty Spell Pack 1.0.0, supplied locally by the user.
Engine: OpenMW 0.52.0 development, revision `73765c58a2`.
Executable SHA256: `B94A8513AA011E7A0F12A15A86A0609B5F68D1D2E269D148148D771BA7DB3B15`.

The integration applies effect index 0 of `nsp_wildintervention` through
`Actor.activeSpells(self):add` after the usual failed-cast detection and chance
roll. It checks that `NiftySpellPack.omwaddon` is enabled and the spell record
exists. If unavailable, it dispatches Arcane Misfires' own intervention through
global and player events. No Nifty code or assets are imported or bundled.

Source inspection confirms Nifty chooses an exterior 2-6 cells from the caster
or an exterior connected to the caster's interior through load doors. It searches
for dry terrain and can use an unlocked, unscripted load door in the destination.
Its player script adjusts landing height and reduces fatigue to a random -15 to
5 outside god mode, with a small chance of an airborne arrival. Its global script
checks whether teleportation is enabled. Arcane Misfires delegates these rules
to the installed mod when available. Its standalone equivalent uses layered
graph traversal, partial Fisher-Yates cell sampling, reservoir selection of
eligible doors, and a separate collision-landing pass to implement the same
rule set through original code. Random draws are consumed differently, so the
same seed need not produce the same destination as Nifty.

## Results

- 67 Lua, record, and package regression tests passed. Coverage includes all
  three teleport effects, multi-effect spells, toggle default and save migration,
  absent content, absent spell record, disabled toggle, zero chance, successful
  casts, unrelated Mysticism spells, experience, feedback, and backfire count.
  Standalone rule checks cover cyclic and disconnected interiors, depth limits,
  distinct cell sampling, regions, water-only searches, ESM4 grid sizing, locked
  and scripted door exclusions, orientation reversal, dry/wet/no-hit landings,
  airborne probability boundary and height, god mode, and stale event protection.
- Native installed-mod run: genuine failed Recall, Divine Intervention, and
  Almsivi Intervention each produced exactly one miscast and Nifty teleport
  event. Each moved the player and reduced fatigue to at most 5. Disabled toggle
  produced the usual Drain Magicka; zero chance produced no further miscast.
- Native absent-mod run: all three genuine failures triggered Arcane Misfires'
  own intervention, moved the player, and reduced fatigue to at most 5.
  Disabled-toggle and zero-chance assertions also passed.
- Native teleport-disabled protection passed: a further failed Recall rolled
  a miscast without teleporting or applying intervention fatigue loss.
- Both native runs exited normally without Lua or engine errors.

The fixture starts in Arrille's Tradehouse and spaces its teleport checks seven
seconds apart. It records fatigue loss, then restores test fatigue after four
seconds so knockdown recovery cannot consume the next automated cast request.
Earlier runs without this recovery step sometimes missed the next casting
animation. This fixture accommodation does not introduce a gameplay cooldown.

Native logs are generated by `tools/verify_nifty_intervention.py` under
`reports/nifty-installed/result.txt` and `reports/nifty-absent/result.txt`.
The automated check requires a local Nifty installation only for the installed
case. Test fixtures and third-party content are excluded from the gameplay ZIP.

## Remaining Coverage

Real-save reload, full modlists, future Nifty releases, and real-engine ESM4
worldspaces remain untested. Disconnected interiors and forced door/airborne
branches have deterministic Lua coverage but have not been separately forced
in the native engine. Native tests establish normal teleport and fatigue
dispatch for both providers and teleport-disabled protection.
